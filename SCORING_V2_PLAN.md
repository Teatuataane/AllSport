# Scoring v2 — decisions from the grill (Tāne, 15 Oct 2026)

Colours stay the only progression. Underneath them every score gets an
**exact score** on the same 0–12 scale, and game totals, contests, calibration,
groups and the season reset are built on it. Every decision below was put to
Tāne one at a time and agreed. This is still play testing; the first official
pilot is 2027, so rules change as each step ships.

## Decisions

1. **Exact score = position between today's colour lines.** 35 reps between
   Kōwhai (30) and Kākāriki (40) is 4.5 (Kōwhai is rung 4). Colours, standards sheets and age shifts
   are unchanged: the colour held is the whole part. Above the top line the score
   keeps rising by the size of the last step (capped at 12 for totals).
2. **Exact scores decide game places.** Game colour totals and season places use
   exact scores; domain and overall show the exact average ("Kōwhai 5.8") while
   the colour itself is still rounded down. All 2026 game totals are recalculated
   (decision 16).
3. **Two-part events take the lower part.** Load and time (or load and reps,
   load and metres) are each placed on their own ladder; the exact score is the
   lower of the two. Replaces the half-step credit.
4. **Contests: the head-to-head rating colour is exact** (1,150 = 8.5). The
   game-day 6/5/4 rule is REPLACED by 4d.
   - 4a/4b. Each contest in a game runs as a **Swiss** tournament, paired on
     demand ("ready" → best free opponent), the app tells players whom to find
     (photo + first name), both confirm the result, kaiwhakawā can settle/override.
   - 4c/4d. The game-day score is the player's **performance rating** for that
     game on the colour scale: 1,100 = 7 (Poroporo), 100 points a colour, 1,600 =
     12, floor 4. Swiss standings give the podium. Conferred rating colours still
     need 10 rated games.
5. **Age:** keep the current bands for the first build; a smooth age curve comes
   later from calibration data.
6. **Calibration target:** among players who have done an event a few times,
   about half reach Kōwhai (4), a third Kahurangi (6), one in ten Hiriwa. Data sets Mā to
   about Hiriwa; Kōura to Taniwha stay pinned to outside elite benchmarks. An
   event needs 20+ players before data overrides the sheet. Data suggests;
   Tāne decides in the sheet.
7. **Standards change once a year** (report early December, live 1 January),
   except obvious mistakes.
8. **Yearly reset stays.** Soften it with a ghost marker of last year's exact
   score per domain on HOME, and a lifetime honours list on the profile.
9. **PRs counted:** "N PRs today" on the game report, a season count on HOME
   (into honours). Display only. A first-ever attempt is not a PR.
10. **Groups** (school / club / whānau): a player may belong to one of each type;
    the group leader approves members (teachers can add a class).
11. **Group board ranks by average improvement per active member** (exact
    overall now vs first game of the season; active = 3+ games this season).
    Shows active members and games beside the rank, not counted.
12. **Privacy:** public group board shows groups only; members and leader see
    names inside; under-13 school students are "First L." on the public board or
    hidden; teachers never see bodyweights or wellbeing.
13. **Matches per contest:** kaiwhakawā's choice per game, default 3; extra
    matches allowed and count.
14. **Contact safety pairing.** Full rules (same age group U12 / 12–15 / 16+,
    within ~15% of the day's bodyweight used privately, mixed sex for U12 only
    unless the kaiwhakawā allows, kaiwhakawā override, no opponent = bye = draw)
    for Wrestling, Mas Wrestling, Arm Wrestling, Kabaddi, Tae Kwon Do, Tug of War.
    Age group only for Fencing, Tag, Rats & Rabbits, Capture the Flag. No limits
    for Speed Chess, the four Sprints, Beach Flags, T-Race, Obstacle Course.
15. **Races run in heats of 4–6** grouped Swiss-style; a heat place counts as
    one-on-one results into the performance rating. **Team games** get teams
    balanced by rating, reshuffled each round; each player's result is against
    the other team's average rating.
16. **Recalculate 2026** game totals on the new rules (past contests keep 6/5/4:
    there is no bracket to rate).

## Build order

3. Calibration report script (read-only; can run in parallel). Due early Dec.

5. One-on-one contests: Swiss on demand, performance rating, safety rules.

7. Smooth age curve from a season of calibration data.
