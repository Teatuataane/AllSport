# The 151 roster: drafts of the 15 new events

**Drafted by Claude, 29 Oct 2026. UNREVIEWED.** Change anything you like, the
same way as the review sheets: type over a level or a value, delete a line, or
leave a note on a line starting with `>`. Once you're happy, these go into
`EVENT_DIFFICULTY_REVIEW.md` and `GRADING_STANDARDS_REVIEW.md` and are compiled
into the app.

How a standard is written (as in the standards sheet): `D3 · 20` is twenty reps
on level D3, `D3 · 30s` a hold that long, `D3 · ≤ 12s` D3 done in 12 seconds or
faster, `D3 · any` D3 done at all. `0.5× BW` is half your bodyweight on the day.

Every event has a first level a beginner can score, so nobody is shut out of
a domain.

Weighted Tuck and Bent Wrist Stretch are drafted from Tāne's descriptions of
29 Oct.

## 1. Maximal Strength

### Wrestler Pullover

*Scored by: heaviest weight lifted, as an estimated one-rep max (like every lift)*

**Levels:** NONE

**How to perform:** Lie on your back on the floor with a barbell on the floor
behind your head. Grip it about shoulder width with your elbows bent. Keeping
the elbows bent and the bar close to your face, pull it over and down until it
touches your chest. That is one rep. Lower it back behind your head under
control.

**Rules:** Shoulders and head stay on the floor. The bar must touch the chest.
No press: the lift ends at the chest. Hips may lift.

> A wrestler's pullover is a bent-arm pull over the face, so it is lighter than
> Pullover & Press. Standards set at about 0.9 of the old Pullover & Press
> ratios. Women's are 0.7 of the men's, the same gap as Pullover & Press.

**Men:** Kiwikiwi 0.05× BW · Whero 0.1× · Karaka 0.2× · Kōwhai 0.35× ·
Kākāriki 0.5× · Kahurangi 0.6× · Poroporo 0.7× · Parahi 0.8× · Hiriwa 0.9× ·
Kōura 1× · Uenuku 1.1× · Taniwha 1.2× BW

**Women:** Kiwikiwi 0.035× BW · Whero 0.07× · Karaka 0.14× · Kōwhai 0.25× ·
Kākāriki 0.35× · Kahurangi 0.42× · Poroporo 0.5× · Parahi 0.55× · Hiriwa 0.65× ·
Kōura 0.7× · Uenuku 0.75× · Taniwha 0.85× BW

### Plate Hold

*Scored by: heaviest load, then longest hold (like Glute Thrust)*

**Levels:** NONE

**How to perform:** Pinch two weight plates together in one hand, smooth sides
out, fingers on one side and thumb on the other. Lift them off the floor to
your side, arm straight, and hold as long as you can.

**Rules:** One hand. Fingers and thumb only: the palm may not wrap the rim.
Chalk is allowed, straps are not. The load is the total weight of the plates in
that hand. Time stops when the plates touch anything.

> Loads are in kilograms, not a share of bodyweight: grip barely scales with
> body size. Two 10kg plates (20kg) for 15 seconds is a strong club lifter;
> two 20kg plates is elite.

**Men:** Kiwikiwi 5kg · 15s · Whero 7.5kg · Karaka 10kg · Kōwhai 12.5kg ·
Kākāriki 15kg · Kahurangi 20kg · Poroporo 25kg · Parahi 30kg · Hiriwa 35kg ·
Kōura 40kg · Uenuku 45kg · Taniwha 50kg (all · 15s)

**Women:** Kiwikiwi 2.5kg · 15s · Whero 5kg · Karaka 7.5kg · Kōwhai 10kg ·
Kākāriki 12.5kg · Kahurangi 15kg · Poroporo 17.5kg · Parahi 20kg ·
Hiriwa 25kg · Kōura 27.5kg · Uenuku 30kg · Taniwha 35kg (all · 15s)

### Weighted Tuck

*Scored by: heaviest weight lifted, as an estimated one-rep max (like every lift)*

**Levels:** NONE

**How to perform:** Hang from a bar with straight arms, a dumbbell held
between your feet. Bring your knees up until they touch your chest, then lower
until your legs are straight again. That is one rep.

**Rules:** Start and finish every rep from a still, straight-legged hang. The
knees must touch the chest. No swinging or kipping. The load is the dumbbell
(or ankle weights) only, not your bodyweight. A dropped weight ends the set.

> Ranks on estimated 1RM from load and reps, like the other lifts, so it joins
> `enforce_lift_estimate`. Loads are a share of bodyweight. Women's are 0.7 of
> the men's, the usual gap for an upper-body lift.

**Men:** Kiwikiwi 0.025× BW · Whero 0.05× · Karaka 0.075× · Kōwhai 0.1× ·
Kākāriki 0.15× · Kahurangi 0.2× · Poroporo 0.25× · Parahi 0.3× · Hiriwa 0.35× ·
Kōura 0.4× · Uenuku 0.45× · Taniwha 0.5× BW

**Women:** Kiwikiwi 0.02× BW · Whero 0.035× · Karaka 0.05× · Kōwhai 0.07× ·
Kākāriki 0.1× · Kahurangi 0.14× · Poroporo 0.175× · Parahi 0.21× ·
Hiriwa 0.25× · Kōura 0.28× · Uenuku 0.315× · Taniwha 0.35× BW

## 2. Calisthenics

### Nordic Curl

*Scored by: difficulty level, then most reps*

**Levels:**
- D1: Band Assisted   (A band from above under the chest takes some of your weight)
- D2: Hand Catch   (Lower as slowly as you can, catch yourself on your hands, push back up)
- D3: Half Nordic   (Lower to halfway and curl back up without the hands)
- D4: Full Nordic   (Lower until the chest nearly touches the floor and curl back up without the hands)
- D5: Arms Overhead   (A full Nordic with the arms straight overhead)

**How to perform:** Kneel on a pad with your ankles held down by a partner or
a fixed bar. Keep a straight line from knees to head. Lower yourself forward
under control, then pull yourself back up with your hamstrings.

**Rules:** Hips stay straight throughout: no bending at the waist. A rep counts
only at your level's depth and back to upright.

**Everyone:** Kiwikiwi D1 · 3 · Whero D1 · 8 · Karaka D1 · 15 · Kōwhai D2 · 3 ·
Kākāriki D2 · 8 · Kahurangi D3 · 3 · Poroporo D3 · 8 · Parahi D4 · 1 ·
Hiriwa D4 · 3 · Kōura D4 · 6 · Uenuku D5 · 1 · Taniwha D5 · 3

## 3. Power

### Discus

*Scored by: furthest throw*

**Levels:** NONE (one implement: 2kg for men, 1kg for women and juniors)

**How to perform:** From standing or a spin inside a 2.5m circle, throw the
discus as far as you can into the sector.

**Rules:** Stay in the circle until the discus lands, then leave from the back
half. It must land inside the sector. Measured from the front of the circle to
the nearest mark. Best of three throws.

> Women's distances are about 0.85 of the men's with the lighter discus.

**Men:** Kiwikiwi 5m · Whero 10m · Karaka 15m · Kōwhai 20m · Kākāriki 25m ·
Kahurangi 30m · Poroporo 35m · Parahi 40m · Hiriwa 45m · Kōura 50m ·
Uenuku 55m · Taniwha 60m

**Women:** Kiwikiwi 4m · Whero 8.5m · Karaka 13m · Kōwhai 17m · Kākāriki 21m ·
Kahurangi 25.5m · Poroporo 30m · Parahi 34m · Hiriwa 38m · Kōura 42.5m ·
Uenuku 47m · Taniwha 51m

## 4. Speed

### Sprint Race

*Scored by: win, draw or loss against another player. The time is kept but does not rank.*

**Levels:** NONE (a pure contest, like the four sprints it replaces)

**How to perform:** Race the other players over the distance the kaiwhakawā
sets on the day, from 50m to 800m.

**Rules:** Start from standing on the call. Stay in your lane on a track, or
your line on grass. First across the line wins.

**Standards:** Rating only, as for every pure contest. Every colour comes from
the head-to-head rating.

> Production check, 29 Oct: no game result has ever been scored under 100m,
> 200m, 400m or 800m Sprint, so there is no history to carry over. The colours
> citing them stay, as for any removed event.

### Hurdles

*Scored by: difficulty level, then fastest time (a timed effort)*

**Levels:**
- D1: Mini Hurdles   (15cm)
- D2: Low Hurdles   (50cm)
- D3: Intermediate Hurdles   (76cm)
- D4: High Hurdles   (91cm)

**How to perform:** Run 60m over five hurdles set at your level's height,
13m from the start and 8.5m apart.

**Rules:** Knocking a hurdle over is allowed. Going round one, or trailing a
leg beside it, is not, and the run doesn't count.

> A ladder, not a contest, so a beginner can score on mini hurdles. Women's
> times are 1.1 of the men's.

**Men:** Kiwikiwi D1 · finish · Whero D1 · ≤ 14s · Karaka D1 · ≤ 12s ·
Kōwhai D2 · finish · Kākāriki D2 · ≤ 12s · Kahurangi D2 · ≤ 11s ·
Poroporo D3 · ≤ 11s · Parahi D3 · ≤ 10s · Hiriwa D3 · ≤ 9.5s ·
Kōura D4 · ≤ 10s · Uenuku D4 · ≤ 9s · Taniwha D4 · ≤ 8.5s

**Women:** Kiwikiwi D1 · finish · Whero D1 · ≤ 15.5s · Karaka D1 · ≤ 13s ·
Kōwhai D2 · finish · Kākāriki D2 · ≤ 13s · Kahurangi D2 · ≤ 12s ·
Poroporo D3 · ≤ 12s · Parahi D3 · ≤ 11s · Hiriwa D3 · ≤ 10.5s ·
Kōura D4 · ≤ 11s · Uenuku D4 · ≤ 10s · Taniwha D4 · ≤ 9.5s

## 6. Endurance

### Underwater Swim

*Scored by: furthest distance on one breath*

**Levels:** NONE

**How to perform:** Push off the wall and swim underwater as far as you can on
a single breath.

**Rules:** No fins. A kaiwhakawā or buddy must watch from the edge the whole
time. The distance ends where your head first breaks the surface. No
hyperventilating before the swim: take at most three normal breaths.

> Breath-holding hardly differs between men and women, so one list for
> everyone. Safety is the first rule: never alone, never hyperventilate.

**Everyone:** Kiwikiwi 5m · Whero 10m · Karaka 15m · Kōwhai 20m ·
Kākāriki 25m · Kahurangi 30m · Poroporo 40m · Parahi 50m · Hiriwa 60m ·
Kōura 75m · Uenuku 90m · Taniwha 100m

## 7. Flexibility

### Cobra Pose

*Scored by: difficulty level, then longest hold*

**Levels:**
- D1: Sphinx   (Lying face down, propped on the forearms, hips on the floor)
- D2: Low Cobra   (Hands under the shoulders, elbows bent, hips on the floor)
- D3: Full Cobra   (Arms straight, hips still on the floor)
- D4: King Cobra   (Full cobra with the knees bent, feet reaching toward the head)
- D5: Head to Feet   (King cobra with the head touching the feet)

**How to perform:** Lie face down and press up into your level's position.
Hold it as long as you can.

**Rules:** Hips stay on the floor at every level. Time stops when you leave
the position.

**Everyone:** Kiwikiwi D1 · any · Whero D1 · 1:00 · Karaka D2 · any ·
Kōwhai D2 · 1:00 · Kākāriki D3 · any · Kahurangi D3 · 1:00 · Poroporo D4 · any ·
Parahi D4 · 30s · Hiriwa D4 · 1:00 · Kōura D5 · any · Uenuku D5 · 30s ·
Taniwha D5 · 1:00

### Butterfly Pose

*Scored by: difficulty level, then longest hold*

**Levels:**
- D1: Upright Butterfly   (Soles together, heels a forearm's length from the hips, back straight)
- D2: Heels In   (Heels pulled in to touch the groin, back straight)
- D3: Elbows to Floor   (Folding forward until both elbows touch the floor)
- D4: Head to Feet   (Folding forward until the forehead touches the feet)
- D5: Chest to Floor   (Chest flat on the floor, knees down)

**How to perform:** Sit with the soles of your feet together and your knees
out to the sides. Take your level's position and hold.

**Rules:** Soles stay together. Hands may hold the feet. Time stops when you
leave the position.

**Everyone:** Kiwikiwi D1 · any · Whero D1 · 1:00 · Karaka D2 · any ·
Kōwhai D2 · 1:00 · Kākāriki D3 · any · Kahurangi D3 · 1:00 · Poroporo D4 · any ·
Parahi D4 · 30s · Hiriwa D4 · 1:00 · Kōura D5 · any · Uenuku D5 · 30s ·
Taniwha D5 · 1:00

### Deep Squat

*Scored by: difficulty level, then longest hold*

**Levels:**
- D1: Supported   (Holding a post, or heels raised on a plate)
- D2: Heels Down   (Heels flat, hips below the knees, hands free)
- D3: Feet Together   (Feet touching, heels flat, hips below the knees)
- D4: Overhead   (Heels flat, feet hip width, a stick held overhead on straight arms)
- D5: Feet Together Overhead   (Feet touching, heels flat, stick overhead)

**How to perform:** Squat as low as you can into your level's position and
hold.

**Rules:** Hips must stay below the knees. Heels stay flat from D2 up. Time
stops when you leave the position.

> Long holds at the low levels: a deep squat is a resting position in much of
> the world, so a short hold would top everyone out at once.

**Everyone:** Kiwikiwi D1 · any · Whero D1 · 2:00 · Karaka D2 · any ·
Kōwhai D2 · 2:00 · Kākāriki D2 · 5:00 · Kahurangi D3 · any · Poroporo D3 · 1:00 ·
Parahi D4 · any · Hiriwa D4 · 1:00 · Kōura D5 · any · Uenuku D5 · 30s ·
Taniwha D5 · 1:00

### Bent Wrist Stretch

*Scored by: difficulty level, then longest hold*

**Levels:**
- D1: Hand Assisted   (The other hand eases the back of the hand toward the forearm)
- D2: Seated   (Sitting back on the heels, backs of the hands on the floor beside the knees, fingers pointing back, elbows bent)
- D3: Kneeling, Arms Straight   (Backs of the hands flat in front of the knees, fingers pointing back, arms straight, hips over the heels)
- D4: Shoulders Over Hands   (Kneeling, backs of the hands flat, shoulders stacked over the wrists)
- D5: Knee Plank   (On the knees with the hips straight, weight forward over the backs of the hands)
- D6: Full Plank   (A full plank on the backs of the hands, fingers pointing back to the feet)

**How to perform:** Place the backs of your hands on the floor, fingernails
down, fingers pointing back toward you. Take your level's position and hold.

**Rules:** The backs of the hands stay flat from D2 up, with the knuckles and
fingernails on the floor. Arms straight from D3 up. Time stops when a hand
lifts or turns.

> Replaces External and Internal Wrist Stretch. Like External Wrist Stretch,
> it works the wrist bending forward, but each level now adds weight through
> the wrist instead of turning the fingers.

**Everyone:** Kiwikiwi D1 · any · Whero D1 · 1:00 · Karaka D2 · any ·
Kōwhai D2 · 1:00 · Kākāriki D3 · any · Kahurangi D3 · 1:00 · Poroporo D4 · any ·
Parahi D4 · 1:00 · Hiriwa D5 · any · Kōura D5 · 1:00 · Uenuku D6 · any ·
Taniwha D6 · 30s

### Shoulder Dislocate (returning)

*Scored by: difficulty level, then most reps*

**Levels:**
- D1: Band   (A resistance band or towel, any width)
- D2: Stick, Very Wide   (Hands twice shoulder width apart)
- D3: Stick, Wide   (Hands 1.75 times shoulder width)
- D4: Stick, Medium   (Hands 1.5 times shoulder width)
- D5: Stick, Narrow   (Hands 1.25 times shoulder width)

**How to perform:** Hold the band or stick in front of your hips with both
hands at your level's width. With straight arms, take it over your head and
down behind your back, then bring it back to the front. That is one rep.

**Rules:** Arms stay straight the whole way. Hands may not slide wider during
the set. Shoulder width is measured across the outside of the shoulders.

> It used to be scored by grip width in centimetres. Levels by width are
> easier to judge, with reps inside each one. Its three old results (85cm to
> 120cm, two players) can't be read on the new ladder, so they stay as
> history only, as the Duck Walk scores did. The one colour citing it stays.

**Everyone:** Kiwikiwi D1 · 5 · Whero D1 · 15 · Karaka D2 · 5 · Kōwhai D2 · 15 ·
Kākāriki D3 · 5 · Kahurangi D3 · 15 · Poroporo D4 · 5 · Parahi D4 · 10 ·
Hiriwa D4 · 15 · Kōura D5 · 5 · Uenuku D5 · 10 · Taniwha D5 · 15

## 8. Control

### Tī Rākau

*Scored by: difficulty level, then most clean rounds*

**Levels:**
- D1: Solo Pattern   (One pair of sticks: tap, flip and catch in rhythm, alone)
- D2: Partner Toss   (With a partner, one stick each, tossed across at the same time)
- D3: Partner Two Sticks   (With a partner, both sticks each, tossed across together)
- D4: Four Square   (Four players in a square, sticks passed around in rhythm)
- D5: Song Pattern   (The full pattern kept in time with a waiata)

**How to perform:** Kneel facing your partner or group with your rākau. Run
your level's pattern in time and count the clean rounds before a stick drops
or the rhythm breaks.

**Rules:** A round counts only if every stick is caught and the rhythm holds.
At D2 and up the score is shared by the partners or the group.

> Each level is a first guess at the progression; please reshape it to the
> patterns you actually teach.

**Everyone:** Kiwikiwi D1 · 3 · Whero D1 · 10 · Karaka D2 · 3 · Kōwhai D2 · 10 ·
Kākāriki D3 · 3 · Kahurangi D3 · 10 · Poroporo D4 · 3 · Parahi D4 · 6 ·
Hiriwa D4 · 10 · Kōura D5 · 1 · Uenuku D5 · 2 · Taniwha D5 · 3

## 10. Aim

### Goal Kicking

*Scored by: difficulty level, then most goals from 10 kicks*

**Levels:**
- D1: In Front, 15m
- D2: In Front, 22m
- D3: Angle, 22m   (15m in from the touchline)
- D4: In Front, 40m
- D5: Sideline, 22m   (On the touchline)

**How to perform:** Take 10 place kicks or drop kicks at the posts from your
level's spot. Count the goals.

**Rules:** The ball must pass between the posts and over the crossbar. A tee
is allowed. Same spot for all 10 kicks.

**Everyone:** Kiwikiwi D1 · 2 · Whero D1 · 5 · Karaka D1 · 8 · Kōwhai D2 · 3 ·
Kākāriki D2 · 6 · Kahurangi D3 · 3 · Poroporo D3 · 6 · Parahi D4 · 2 ·
Hiriwa D4 · 5 · Kōura D5 · 2 · Uenuku D5 · 4 · Taniwha D5 · 6
